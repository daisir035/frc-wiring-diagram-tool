# CANcoder Artwork

The wired CANcoder icon is a top-view schematic of the clear ABS housing, not a through-bore encoder or rectangular controller board.

Reference features:

- Rounded head and narrower rectangular cable tail.
- Diagonally opposed mounting ears. Their top-view orientation is mirrored from the manufacturer's bottom-view drawing.
- Translucent housing, green CTR mark and small cover screw boss. The center is not an open shaft bore.
- Three pairs of leads, retaining all existing port IDs, positions and directions.
